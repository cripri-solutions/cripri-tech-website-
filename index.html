About CRIPRI

We turn practical ideas into digital solutions.

CRIPRI is a technology initiative focused on building accessible, purposeful software and exploring technology that responds to the needs of people and institutions.

Our commitment

We believe good technology should make work clearer, information easier to manage and services more accessible. Our work is guided by real use cases, continuous learning and a focus on solutions that can grow with the people who use them.
